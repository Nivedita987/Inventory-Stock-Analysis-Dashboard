# 📦 Inventory & Stock Analysis Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to monitor inventory levels, stock availability, product movement, and reorder requirements.

The dashboard provides a simple view of inventory performance to help identify low-stock products and understand stock availability across different shelves and products.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides key inventory insights through:

- 📥 **Total Received** – Total quantity of products received.
- 📤 **Total Dispatched** – Total quantity of products dispatched.
- 📦 **Quantity Available** – Current quantity available in inventory.
- 📈 **Stock Availability Trend** – Monthly trend of available stock.
- ⚠️ **Bottom 10 Stock Quantity** – Products with the lowest available stock.
- 🔄 **Reorder Level** – Comparison of available quantity with reorder requirements.
- 📊 **Stock Availability Percentage** – Overall percentage of available stock.
- 🔎 **Product Selector** – Filter dashboard information by product.
- 🗄️ **Shelf Selector** – Analyze inventory by shelf.

## 📌 Dashboard Features

### KPI Summary
Provides an overview of received, dispatched, and available inventory.

### Stock Availability Trend
Shows the change in available inventory across months.

### Low Stock Analysis
Displays the bottom 10 products based on available quantity along with their reorder levels.

### Interactive Filters
Users can filter the dashboard by product and shelf to perform more detailed analysis.

### Stock Availability
A percentage-based visual provides a quick overview of overall inventory availability.
